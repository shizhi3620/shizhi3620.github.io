I wanted a place that moves more slowly than a chat thread: somewhere to keep ideas worth considering again, whether they come from a change in how I work, an uncertainty during a job search, or a judgment that gradually takes shape in daily life.

This is not an attempt to present a complete personal brand or turn every conclusion into advice. Most posts are working notes from a particular moment: what I noticed, what supports it, and what may need to change later. Publishing them asks me to separate facts, feelings, and guesses before I write, and gives my future self a way to revisit the decisions behind them.

Chinese is the source language here. English posts are translated with AI assistance and reviewed by the author before publication; both versions should describe the same facts. When a translation is not ready, the Chinese post may still be published and the unavailable language is shown clearly.

Over time, I hope this blog becomes a dependable index: a record of both the questions I care about and the context in which my conclusions formed.
